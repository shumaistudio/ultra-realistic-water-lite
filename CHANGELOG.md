# Changelog

## 1.3-lite.1
- Rebased on Realistic Water 1.3 (upstream removed its built-in shader packs in favour of Vitrail and added rain, boiling/steam, portals, Sodium menu, ...).
- Lite features carried over: simple translucent shading, water colour/opacity/metallic, quality presets, adaptive budget, chunk scans per tick.
- Own "Water Settings..." button in Video Settings (upstream's Video Settings hook was removed); Sodium menu pages are translated.
- Japanese translation extended to the settings new in 1.3 (labels, categories, new items/death messages).

## 1.2.3-lite.3
- Default water colour set to R 0 / G 53 / B 148, opacity 0.27, metallic shine 0.2.
- Japanese (ja_jp) translation: blocks/items, settings screen (all setting names, categories, cost labels, descriptions of the lite settings).
- Settings screen text now goes through the language files; missing translations fall back to English.

## 1.2.3-lite.2
- Flat colour water: no depth haze or blur. Settings for water RGB, opacity and a cheap metallic shine.
- "Water Settings..." button in Options > Video Settings.

## 1.2.3-lite.1
- Simple translucent shading (no reflection, refraction, ripples, sun glint, foam lace).
- Quality presets (Potato / Low / Medium / Original), adaptive simulation budget, chunk scans per tick.
- Lighter defaults, whitewater off by default, mesh detail changes apply immediately.
- Renamed to "Ultra Realistic Water lite".
